NOVA modifications: selected C-note samples and four kit sounds; decoded MP3 where applicable, downmixed to mono, resampled to 24 kHz 16-bit PCM, bounded to 0.5–5 seconds, final 30 ms fade. No claims of studio-library quality or physical listening acceptance. Piano/bass have one recorded dynamic layer; MIDI velocity scales gain. Strings are a finite ensemble recording, without looping or a true legato transition engine. Kit supports GM keys 36, 38, 42, 46; other keys reject adoption and retain the current sound. Original MIDI remains unchanged.

Manifest.json records file sizes, SHA256, immutable source URLs and source SHA256. Imported PCM plus provenance is stored in each project and included in JSON/complete backups, avoiding future network dependence for saved songs. HTTP serving, native HTTPS/file handling and Safari acceptance still require verification. Existing Synth remains selectable.
